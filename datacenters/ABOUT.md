# About

The Open Data Center Atlas tracks large data center projects: hyperscale and AI campuses,
conversions of industrial and crypto-mining sites, and major colocation developments. Explore
where projects are being built, the organizations involved, their capacity and their progress.

## Using the data

- Numbers, dates, roles and statuses link to the public sources that reported them.
- Power figures include their basis, scope and date so you can distinguish planned capacity
  from operating capacity and compare figures with the same meaning.
- Missing values are blank. They do not mean zero.
- Data is free to download without an account. Corrections and record history are available
  in the public repository.
