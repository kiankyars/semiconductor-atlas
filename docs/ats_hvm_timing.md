## Scope and chronology decisions

- AT&S Kulim is not Infineon Kulim. Match the new substrate project's HVM
  commencement, not its full ramp or the separate second Kulim plant. The
  follow-up labels investment as phase 1; the original target has no phase
  number. No all-phase completion or capacity quantity is inferred.
- Leoben's original HVM target is not retroactively removed because its later
  description emphasizes R&D and small volume. That scope tension remains
  explicit and its HVM outcome remains unknown.
- The May 15 Leoben page's June 2025 opening label is still future guidance at
  that document date. July repeats the opening label after June. Neither is
  the HVM event. End-2024 sample shipments and generic ramp statements are
  also different evidence, not HVM realization or proof of nonoccurrence.
- The October 31, 2024 release predicts later HVM at both plants. It is a
  guidance revision, not an actual delay duration, observation of
  nonoccurrence, cancellation or replacement for the original interval.
- Equipment-installation percentages, cleanroom area, staffing, investment,
  customer names and chart values are not throughput or usable capacity.
