# Dresden and Kulim timing screen, September 9, 2026

## Selection and exposure

Two purposively selected new-fab projects, not a publisher-complete population or
an announcement-time sample. The reviewer had already seen successful-opening
leads before selecting these projects. Research followed the existing Villach
example to improve event-specific outcome coverage. It is exposed retrospective
research, not historical Atlas forecasting, blind evaluation or model training.

The target roster covers all prospective physical project-event timing statements
for these two projects in the four selected guidance releases: Dresden May 2024
and February 2025; Kulim February 2022 and May 2023. Repeated wording for one event
within a source is one case. Supplier deliveries, government funding, group
revenue/market-share/capacity targets, sustainability goals and other sites are
outside this declared event screen. Historical construction descriptions are
context, not additional prospective targets. This is not complete revision history.

The three other acquired documents were selected as possible outcome/context
sources, not as an additional target population. In particular, the prospectus's
Dresden guidance was not added after exposure to its content. No prior review,
PMT cohort, forecast vintage or prediction journal is modified.
