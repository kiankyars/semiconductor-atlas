# Exposed project-guidance pairing screen

This is a purposive screen of three candidates surfaced during the September 8-9,
2026 research, after reviewers had seen possible outcomes. It is not an announcement-
time population, an independently complete publisher census, a blind holdout or a
historical Atlas prediction vintage. Every candidate below remains in the screen.

## Infineon new Villach fab

Retained issuer Q1 FY2021 release, visibly dated February 4, 2021, and Austria
FY2021 release, visibly dated December 7, 2021. The former places startup in the
last fiscal quarter; the latter explicitly reports August production startup of
the new 300-mm fab. The review separately verifies the fiscal calendar and keeps
the new project distinct from the established Villach campus and Dresden network.
These are eligible for a descriptive source-statement comparison only. Exact
historical byte availability is unverified: the PDF metadata records modification
in May 2024. The current issuer is also the outcome reporter.

The February target is a revised schedule, not the original announcement target.
This screen does not claim to enumerate every Villach revision. Earlier reported
readiness dates are not silently converted into production-start targets.
