## Target scope and evidence method

The target scope is explicit source-stated calendar targets for construction completion,
opening, production start or operational state in the reviewed project narrative and project
timeline sections. Investment, jobs, technology descriptions, undated aspirations, conditional
future output rates and historical milestone statements are not additional dated planned
targets. This is not an exhaustive inventory of every claim or every external publication.

The paired source texts were inspected by document, and the root re-read the retained target
and scope passages. Source-body bindings and selected fragment/context hashes are checked
against every applicable actual predecessor and after body. Label spans are zero-based UTF-8
bytes with exclusive ends. They are not character offsets or normalized-text offsets. The
labels reproduce only short temporal literals, exact locators and hashes, not full articles.
No new publisher retrieval or source permission is asserted.

These are manual semantic annotations. Byte binding verifies where a judgment points, not
whether a reviewer chose the correct subject, scope, target or verdict. No canonical facility
identity, physical attainment, accepted claim, alert outcome or investment signal follows.
