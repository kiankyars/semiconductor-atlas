The current [Intel terms](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/legal/terms-of-use.html)
are dated June 21, 2024. Sections 4.1.14, 4.1.16 and 4.1.17 restrict automated
search/scraping, mining/metadata harvesting and systematic collection. Section 8.1
has a limited personal informational-document allowance, conditional on the other
terms; it is not blanket permission for this intelligence collection workflow.
The older terms still visible on an API-portal page must not replace this review.

The intc.com disclaimer and release footer expose a QuoteMedia market-data terms
link, not a clear Intel document-retention grant. The reviewed Intel.com terms
apply where posted or referenced; their exact applicability to intc.com has not
been established. Neither that uncertainty nor the empty HTTP-200 robots response
authorizes collection. The one robots request returned no body and no usable
directives. No source-document bytes were downloaded locally, no capture plan or
collector was enabled, and no alternate issuer host was used after this decision.
No permission request was sent. Revisit only with a documented permitted basis,
not another mirror or an older policy.
