## ECA: no scorable same-event timing pair

The [ECA Special Report 12/2025](https://www.eca.europa.eu/ECAPublications/SR-2025-12/SR-2025-12_EN.pdf)
has a purposive ten-project audit sample, not a comprehensive manufacturing
population. Annex I describes its selection; Annex III mixes research, pilots
and manufacturing. The main reviewer and a separate agent inspected its text.
The PDF skill prompted complete-page screenshot checks, but those calls returned
timeouts or no model-visible image. Visual confirmation is not claimed.

No Annex III entry provides an earlier calendar manufacturing target paired with
a comparable realized event. In particular, paragraph 7's operational statement
is an upper bound, not a December occurrence interval; its retrospective
construction duration is not earlier guidance. Paragraph 10 has a prospective
production target, not an observed outcome. Anonymous aggregate FOAK counts do
not identify project-level cancellations, and a halt is not cancellation.

Decision: do not create another zero-score diagnostic from this source. The
English HTML route was refused by the web tool and was not retried; the separate
published PDF was readable as text. No local acquisition was performed.

## Federal Reserve: separate aggregate-production candidate

The Board's [G.17 download documentation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/download.htm)
describes monthly text data and series documentation. The [current supplement
dictionary](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/Current/ipdisk/g17sup_tab1.txt)
identifies G3344 as semiconductor **and other electronic component** manufacturing,
NAICS 3344. It is not a semiconductor-only, leading-edge, HBM or packaging series.
An industrial-production index is not wafers, chips, installed capacity or
economically usable facility output. No allocation to companies or sites is supported.

The [release-date archive](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/default.htm)
links dated data/documentation pages. The [January 15, 2021 page](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/20210115/ipdisk/alltables.htm)
explicitly describes data through December 2020. This is a promising route for
publisher-vintage backtests; the linked data bytes, period coverage, code/base
changes and historical revision behavior have not been inspected or verified.
Using today's revised series truncated at old dates would not establish a
historically available input set. The special real-time-estimates file described
on the download page covers broad aggregates, not a documented G3344 series.

The [Board disclaimer](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) generally
places Board information in the public domain with attribution, subject to
marked third-party and insignia exceptions. Its
[website policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/website-linking-policies.htm)
prohibits interference and security circumvention. The robots URL returned a
non-retryable web-tool refusal, not usable policy text. It was not retried through
another tool. No raw download or acquisition approval follows from this screen.

Decision: retain as a possible separately labeled macro-production benchmark,
not a replacement for facility-level forecasting. An optional user preference
question is outstanding; do not silently broaden the active work into this
benchmark. Facility outcomes, held-out evaluation and calibrated forecasts remain
open regardless of this lead.
